\subsection{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

% TODO: Interpretazione risultati

The experimental results reveal clear trade-offs between the two arithmetic backends:

\textbf{QFT-based backend}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Fewer qubits (no explicit carry qubits)
    \item Generally shallower circuits
    \item Relies on controlled phase rotation gates
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Ripple-carry backend}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Additional qubits required for carry propagation
    \item Deeper circuits due to sequential carry chain
    \item Uses CNOT and Toffoli (T) gates
\end{itemize}

The choice of backend depends on the target quantum hardware. Systems with high-fidelity rotation gates may favor QFT-based arithmetic, while systems with efficient Toffoli implementations may prefer ripple-carry.
